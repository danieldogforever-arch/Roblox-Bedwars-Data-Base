How the math works

  • Armor: leather 36%, iron 56%, diamond 76%, emerald 84% damage reduction.
  • Armor penetration ignores part of the armor. Example: 40 damage on leather does 25.6, or 29.91 with 40% penetration (Ares's Spear).
  • Team damage upgrades don't stack: +5%, +10% or +20%, whichever is highest.
  • The team armor upgrade takes another 10/20/30% off after armor, and it works with no armor too. Armor penetration doesn't affect it. Example: an Iron Sword (30) on leather with +10% does 17.28.
  • Damage buffs multiply together. Example: Noctium Blade IV (47) with the +20% upgrade does 56.4, and 67.68 with Void Ascension on top.
  • Swords have a 0.3 second cooldown, and every crossbow and headhunter has 1.3 seconds. The headhunter does 55 (82.5 headshot), and the team damage upgrade works on projectiles too.
  • Forest's extra damage is reduced by armor like a normal hit (no armor penetration). Static's 15 lands at the same time as its 5. Forest's damage can vary slightly.
  • Soul Reaver is counted as if you just got a kill, so the aura runs for its 14 ticks (7 seconds) from your first hit.
  • With an enchant, hits to kill and time to kill come from simulating the fight: you swing as fast as your weapon allows, and Fire, Static and Soul Reaver tick in between. Each block in the bar is everything dealt from one hit to the next.
  • Critical Strike: 20-45% more damage in whole percents. "Average" uses a 40% crit chance and a 32.5% average bonus.
  • Berserker (under 25 HP): about 35% more damage at 0 HP, 1% less for every 3 HP you have. It heals about half the bonus damage.
  • Crit stars aren't included. Use "Extra damage" to add them.
  • Hephaestus mechs only reduce projectile damage: Wood 0%, Iron 15%, Diamond 25%, Emerald 30%, Void 40%. Melee hits do full damage to the shield.
  • Gregory's gargoyles change sword damage and speed: Small 70% every 0.36s, Regular 90% every 0.34s, Giant 100% every 0.67s. As a target they have 50, 90 or 200 HP and wear the same armor as Gregory.
  • Infernal Shielder takes 10% less damage, or 40% while holding its shield, which also deflects projectiles.
  • Isabel's shield blocks a set amount of the first hit (10 / 15 / 20 / 30, or all of it with an emerald sword), then needs 10 seconds to come back.
  • Health Regen Axolotl's healing (about 9% HP over 5 seconds) is counted as extra shield.
