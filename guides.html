Best order

For a normal match, starting with no upgrades:

  1. Diamond Gen T13
  2. Team Gen T14
  3. Team Gen T28
  4. Team Gen T316
  5. Armor T14
  6. Armor T28
  7. Damage T15
  8. Diamond Gen T26
  9. Diamond Gen T310
  10. Damage T210
  11. Armor T320
  12. Damage T318

If generators aren't your goal, skip the Gen upgrades and go straight for Armor and Damage. Get Break Speed when your team is going for beds.

Why this order

  • Diamond Gen T1 always comes first. Your base makes no diamonds without it, so Team Gen upgrades do nothing for diamonds until you have it. It pays itself back in about 2 minutes.
  • Rush Team Gen. It speeds up iron for everyone, makes your diamond gen faster, and T3 adds an emerald every 20 seconds. Diamond Gen T1 → Team Gen T1 → T2 → T3 is the fastest way to Team Gen T3 (see Generators).
  • Diamond Gen T2 and T3 come later. Before Team Gen T3 they take 8–14 minutes to pay back, so get your team tankier with Armor T1, T2 and Damage T1 first. After Team Gen T3 they pay back in about 2.5 and 3 minutes.
  • Armor before Damage. 5% and 10% more damage is nothing compared to taking 10% and 20% less. +5% early game won't change any fights.
  • If the game will be short, skip the slow ones (Diamond Gen T2/T3 and every T3) and spend on gear instead.

Fastest way to Team Gen T3

Diamond Gen T1 → Team Gen T1 → Team Gen T2 → Team Gen T3, skipping Diamond Gen T2 and T3 until after. Assuming you get one Diamond Guardian at 3:00 and 8:00 (3 diamonds each):

75% means 3 diamonds every 70 seconds from the 2 mid generators, 50% means 1 every 35 seconds. This is the fastest of every possible order.

Break Speed

Hits to break each block with every tool. Pick a Break Speed tier:

Green is the fastest tool for each block. Pickaxes are for Stone Brick, Ceramic and Obsidian, axes for Oak Plank and Beds, and Shears for Wool. Any other tool does the same small amount (1) to every block, whatever its tier. Break Speed boosts every hit, so even Shears break Ceramic in 6 hits at T3.

Explosives on Obsidian

Obsidian takes 20 TNT or 4 Siege TNT to break. After 3 Siege TNT it's so low that Shears break it in one hit.
